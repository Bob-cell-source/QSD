## Findings

- WISE 2024 Part III, LNCS 15438, contains a dedicated Recommendation Systems section.
- At least fifteen records appear in that section, covering several recommendation settings.
- WISE 2023 also contains a Recommendation Systems section with sequential and session-based recommendation papers.
- WISE 2025 workshop proceedings are indexed in DBLP, but a fully inspectable main-proceedings recommendation section was not found during this search. Therefore, WISE 2024 is used as the latest fully verified main-conference style sample.

## Evidence Boundary

- Published page ranges describe final proceedings papers and do not establish the next edition's submission page limit.
- Only MDAP was available as fully inspectable author-released text in this search; style conclusions for other papers rely on official abstracts, proceedings organization, and metadata.
- Venue style is inferred from multiple recent papers. Springer LNCS formatting requirements come from the official publisher page.
